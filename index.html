ميزات · Features

Everything you need to keep a daily habit

No feed. No noise. One verse, presented the way it deserves, and the tools to understand it deeply.

01

A verse at dawn

Pick your moment: Fajr, sunrise, or last thing at night. Your ayah arrives exactly when you want it, in a notification worth opening.

02

Understand it

40+ translations side by side, with tafsir from Ibn Kathir, as-Sa‘di and more, one tap below every verse.

03

Listen to it

Every verse comes with recitation from renowned qaris. Slow it down, loop it, and let it settle.

04

Reflect on it

A private journal beside every ayah. Write what it means to you today, and Ayah resurfaces it when the verse returns.

05

Widgets & watch

The verse lives on your home screen, lock screen, and wrist, beautiful in light and dark, always readable.

06

Share it as art

Turn the day’s ayah into a card worth sending: to family, to friends, to someone who needs it today.

كيف يعمل · How it works

Three quiet steps

  1. Choose your time

    Pick when your day begins with the Quran. Change it whenever life changes.

  2. Receive your ayah

    Each morning, one verse: chosen in sequence, never random, never repeated mid-journey.

  3. Read · Listen · Reflect

    A minute of stillness: read the translation, hear the recitation, keep a thought.
